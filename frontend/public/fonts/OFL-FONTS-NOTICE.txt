Self-hosted Google Fonts (web woff2 subsets: latin + latin-ext)

  DM Sans              dm-sans-{400,500,700}[-ext].woff2
  Jost                 jost-{300,400}[-ext].woff2
  Bodoni Moda          bodoni-moda-{400,600,700}[-ext].woff2
  Crimson Text         crimson-text-{400,600}[-ext].woff2
  Cormorant SC         cormorant-sc-{400,600}[-ext].woff2
  EB Garamond          eb-garamond-{400,500}[-ext].woff2
  Cormorant Garamond   cormorant-garamond-{400,500}-italic[-ext].woff2,
                       cormorant-garamond-600[-ext].woff2

Source:  https://fonts.google.com
License: SIL Open Font License v1.1 (all families above)
         https://scripts.sil.org/OFL

The `-ext` files carry the latin-ext subset (Hungarian o-double-acute / u-double-acute and
friends); the @font-face rules in frontend/src/index.css gate them with the
matching unicode-range so the browser only fetches latin-ext when needed.

These woff2 files are vendored and served from this app's own origin. We
deliberately do NOT add a Google Fonts <link> or hot-link the gstatic CDN:
self-hosting keeps every visitor request first-party so no IP addresses leak
to a third party (same privacy stance as General Sans, see
GENERAL-SANS-LICENSE.txt).
