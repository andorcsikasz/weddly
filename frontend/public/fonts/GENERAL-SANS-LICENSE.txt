Source:  https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/general-sans
License: ITF Free Font License (FFL) — free for personal AND commercial use.

These woff2 files (weights 300/400/500/600, Latin subset) are vendored and
served from this app's own origin. We deliberately do NOT hot-link the
Fontshare CDN: self-hosting keeps every visitor request first-party so no
IP addresses leak to a third party (same privacy stance as the project's
system-font stacks). This is the only webfont in the project; it is the
"speciality coffee" voice for the landing founders band.
