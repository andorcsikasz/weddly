Noto Sans SC (Simplified Chinese), Regular
Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk (Sans/SubsetOTF/SC/NotoSansSC-Regular.otf)
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License v1.1.

Used by the PDF print pipeline (`backend/src/domain/pdf.ts`) so guest names
with Hungarian / Cyrillic / Greek / Simplified-Chinese glyphs render
correctly. Other scripts (Arabic, Devanagari, Hangul, Hiragana/Katakana,
Traditional Chinese) still fall back to '?' — embedding those subsets is
tracked as a follow-up so the image stays a manageable size.
